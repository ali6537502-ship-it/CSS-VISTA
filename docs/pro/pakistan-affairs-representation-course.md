# Pakistan Movement: representation and reform, 1905–1919

20 October 2026. Private preparation under the owner's release hold. This increment does not deploy code, publish lessons, reopen Pro or activate payments or AI.

## Prepared learning

One further unit extends the existing Pakistan Movement family. Twenty lesson sections each have a reviewed checkpoint; ten different recall questions and one independent writing task support later practice. The sequence covers the Bengal reorganisation and varied responses, Simla representation demands, early Muslim League organisation, separate electorates and weightage, the 1909 framework, the 1911 reversal, the Lucknow scheme, the Montagu declaration and the provincial/central distinctions in the 1919 Act.

The lessons separate voter eligibility from seat shares, proposals from enacted law, and elected participation from control of the executive. Worked hypothetical examples identify their assumptions. Students receive one idea at a time, attempt checkpoints before seeing explanations, retry mistakes, save their own assessment and return for separately scheduled recall. The existing native topic engine supplies these workflows; no parallel account, content or grading system is added. Fixed instruction and checking use no AI.

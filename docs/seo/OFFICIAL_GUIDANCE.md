## Interpretation boundaries

Advertising eligibility and Search indexability are separate decisions. An educational calculator can deserve indexing while remaining ad-free. A downloadable document can remain available even when its catalogue page is deliberately excluded from search. A successful build or crawl cannot determine a historical AdSense review reason or Google's actual indexing totals.

Account-based consent, site ownership, Auto Ads exclusions and review status must be verified in the owner's account. Existing repository documents describing past dashboard settings are historical assertions, not observations made during this audit.

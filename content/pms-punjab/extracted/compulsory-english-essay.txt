                   PUNJAB PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION
         COMBINED COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION (CCE) SYLLABUS

                                     ENGLISH ESSAY


Duration: 02.30 hours
Total Marks: 100


   a. Candidates would be required to write an essay in English of about 1500 words.

   b. The candidates are expected to reflect a clear understanding of the topic, conceptual clarity
      and logical reasoning. Candidates’ articulation, expression and correct use of English

      language will be examined.

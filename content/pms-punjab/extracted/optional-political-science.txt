                   PUNJAB PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION
        COMBINED COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION (CCE) SYLLABUS


                                   POLITICAL SCIENCE
Total Marks: 100

   PART-A: POLITICAL CONCEPTS, POLITICAL INSTITUTIONS, POLITICAL
               REGIMES AND POLITICAL PROCESSES
                                             Topics
1. Political Concepts: State, Sovereignty, Power, Legitimacy, Law, Liberty, Justice and Human
  Rights.
2. Political Institutions: Executive, Legislature and Judiciary.
3. Political Regimes: Forms of Government (Democracy, Authoritarianism, Dictatorship,
  Parliamentary, Presidential, Federal, Unitary and Confederal Systems).

4. Political Processes: Political Culture, Political Socialization, Political Participation, Political
  Development, Representation, Elections, Electoral Systems, Public Opinion, Political Parties,
  Pressure Groups and Lobbies.



PART-B: POLITICAL THEORIES AND POLITICAL IDEOLOGIES
                                            Topics
1. Political Theories: Western Political Thought (Plato, Aristotle, Machiavelli, Hobbes, Locke,
  Rousseau, Montesquieu, Hegel and Marx); Muslim Political Thought (Al-Farabi, Al-Mawardi,
  Al-Ghazali, Ibn Khaldun, Shah Waliullah and Allama Iqbal).
2. Political Ideologies: Idealism, Realism, Liberalism, Capitalism, Utilitarianism, Socialism,
  Marxism, Nationalism, Feminism, Secularism and Islamic Ideology.




PART-C: POLITICAL SYSTEMS AND COMPARATIVE POLITICS
                                               Topics
  1.   Political Systems: Concepts, Structural-Functional Features, Models, Approaches and
       Typology.
  2.   Major Political Systems: United States of America (USA), United Kingdom (UK), China,
       India, Türkiye and Pakistan.

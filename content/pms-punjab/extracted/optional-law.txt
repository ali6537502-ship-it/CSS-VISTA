                 PUNJAB PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION
       COMBINED COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION (CCE) SYLLABUS

                                             LAW

Total Marks: 100

                                    PART-I: CIVIL LAW

   1) The Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 (Act V of 1908).
   2) The Contract Act, 1872.
   3) The Court Fees Act, 1870.
   4) The Punjab Land Revenue Act, 1967.
   5) The Specific Relief Act, 1877.
   6) The Limitation Act, 1908.

                                  PART-II: CRIMINAL LAW

   1) The Pakistan Penal Code, 1860 (Act XLV of 1860).
   2) The Code of Criminal Procedure, 1898 (Act V of 1898).
   3) The Police Order, 2002 (Chief Executive's Order No. 22 of 2002).
   4) The Qanun-e-Shahadat Order, 1984 (P.O. No. 10 of 1984).
   5) The Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act, 2016 (Act XL of 2016), as amended.

                                    PART-III:
              JURISPRUDENCE, CONSTITUTIONAL & ADMINISTRATIVE LAW

   1) Jurisprudence.
   2) Islamic Jurisprudence.
   3) Principles of Constitutional Law and the Constitution of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan,
      1973.
   4) Administrative Law

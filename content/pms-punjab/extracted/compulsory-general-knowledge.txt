                      PUNJAB PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION
        COMBINED COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION (CCE) SYLLABUS

                                  GENERAL KNOWLEDGE
                                              (Objective)

Duration: 90 Minutes
Total Marks: 100
a.    Current Affairs: Candidates are expected to have updated knowledge of the current events,
      issues and developments. They are also expected to know the necessary historical perspective
      needed to understand these events. Candidates shall be examined in the following areas:
      i.)     Pakistan’s economic, social and political issues, regional disparities, water scarcity,
              Cyber laws.
      ii.)    Pakistan’s relations with neighbouring countries.
      iii.)   Pakistan’s relations with the Muslim world.
      iv.)    Pakistan and the UN, European Union and regional organizations (ECO, SAARC, SCO,
              ASEAN and ARF)
      v.)     Pakistan’s relations with the US, China, EU and Russia.
      vi.)    Global Issues: International peace and security; economy; human rights; terrorism; food
              security; energy security; global warming; nuclear issues, including proliferation and
              security; international trade; trade regimes; Sustainable Development Goals, etc.; world
              order; international political economy; international and regional disputes; traditional and
              non-traditional security threats; globalization; the divide between the Global North and
              the Global South; human security; climate change; artificial intelligence; non-state actors;
              scientific and technological advancements; migration; population growth; and
              transformative global trends and patterns.
      vii.) Relations with India, including Kashmir dispute.
      viii.) Issues relating to the Middle East and Southwest Asia.
      ix.)    Prominent personalities, opinion makers, NGOs, INGOs , non-state actors, etc.
      x.)     Globalization.
      xi.)    Evolving socioeconomic and political situation in Pakistan and the world and important
              events.
      xii.) Sports, Music, Culture, Literature.

b.    Physical Sciences:
      i.)   Basic information about the solar system, galaxies and the universe, including common
            astronomical terms.
      ii.)  Natural hazards and disasters e.g. earthquakes, cyclones, tsunamis etc. and disaster risk
            management.
      iii.) Energy sources, both fossil fuels and renewable sources and their sustainable use.
      iv.)  Basics of Physics and Chemistry including atomic structures, chemical bonding,
            radiations, laws and Principles of Physics.
      v.)   Materials, chemicals and their properties.
      vi.)  Minerals and their mining.

c.   Biological Sciences:
     i.)    Fundamentals of living organisms, e.g. cell structure and the nucleus.
     ii.)   Bio-molecular structures like proteins, lipids, carbohydrates, enzymes etc. and their
            functions/properties.
     iii.)  Plant and animal life.
     iv.)   Human body, its parts, organs and their functions.
     v.)    Common diseases, their causes, prevention, vitamins, their sources and functions;
            vaccines, etc.
     vi.)   Ecosystems, ecology and environmental biology.
     vii.) Biodiversity Conservation.
     viii.) Fundamentals of Genetics and Biotechnology.
     ix.)   Regenesis: How Synthetic Biology will reinvent nature and ourselves.
     x.)    The diversity of life.

d.   Environment Studies:
     i.)    Eco-system, biodiversity, climate change, the water cycle, the biosphere, and rock types.
     ii.)   Atmospheric pollution, its types, sources, effects, ozone depletion, greenhouse effects,
            CFCs, etc.
     iii.)  International conventions and regimes on concerning climate change, biodiversity,
            wetlands, desertification, carbon credits etc. and ongoing international commitments on
            Environment.
     iv.)   Types, sources, effects and remedies of water pollution; sustainable use of water;
            recycling; waste management.
     v.)    Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and climate change adaptation and mitigation,
            considering their growing importance in environmental governance and sustainable
            development.
     vi.)   Smog & Air Quality Index (AQI).
     vii.) Water conservation, water scarcity, and virtual water.
     viii.) Renewable energy and energy conservation.

e.   Food Sciences:
     i.)   Diet and essential compounds for health.
     ii.)  Quality of food, preservation, problems relating to the deterioration of fruits and
           vegetables; adulteration.
     iii.) Food security, global issues concerning hunger etc.
     iv.)  Public health nutrition.
     v.)   Food safety, adulteration, and regulation.

f.   Computer Science & Information Technology:
     i.)   Fundamentals of hardware and software.
     ii.)  Data processing, knowledge of MS Word, Excel and PowerPoint.
     iii.) Networking, e-mails, social media.
     iv.)  Basics of telecommunication including GPS, Fiber Optics etc.
     v.)   Artificial Intelligence.
     vi.)  Information security and privacy.
     vii.) Latest developments.

g.        Basic Mathematics:
          i.)   Basic Arithmetic, Algebra and Geometry; problems relating to averages, ratios and
                percentages; geometric figures; sets; equations; areas of plane figures; matrices and
                determinants; arithmetic and geometric sequences etc.
          ii.)  Arithmetical Problems relating to time and work, distance and speed, age, directions etc.
          iii.) Basic statistics.

h.        General:
          i.)   Famous scientists and their claim to fame.
          ii.)  Famous inventions, discoveries, scientific laws, theories, and postulates.
          iii.) Units and instruments of measurement.

i.        English:
          Candidates are expected to have basic knowledge of English grammar, parts of speech,
          prepositions, punctuation, active and passive voice, idioms, proverbs, phrases, synonyms,
          antonyms, analogies, sentence construction etc. Candidates should be able to use English
          correctly.

j.        Urdu:
          The candidates are expected to have a good command over the Urdu language, its correct usage,
          grammar, idioms, and proverbs etc.

k.        Islamic Studies (General Knowledge for non-Muslim candidates):
          The candidates are expected to display basic knowledge of the Holy Quran, Hadith, life and
          times of the Holy Prophet (PBUH), Khulafa-e-Rashideen, Pillars of Islam, Basic Beliefs, broad
          knowledge of important events in Islamic History and prominent personalities. Moreover,
          Non-Muslim candidates have the option either to attempt questions on Islamic Studies or the
          questions pertaining to general knowledge.


Note:       The topics mentioned in the syllabus are only indicative for the general guidance of the
            candidates and cannot be deemed as an exhaustive list.

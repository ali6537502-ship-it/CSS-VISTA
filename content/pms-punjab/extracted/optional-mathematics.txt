                                  MATHEMATICS
Total Marks: 100


                                            Topics


  1.   CALCULUS AND ANALYTICAL MATHEMATICS
         • Differential calculus: limits, continuity, differentiability, the Mean Value
           Theorem, maxima and minima.
         • Integral calculus: indefinite and definite integrals, the Fundamental Theorem
           of Calculus.
         • Sequences and Series: convergence, tests for convergence, absolute and
           conditional convergence, and power series.
         • Vector calculus: vector-valued functions, vector fields, line integrals,
           divergence and curl.
         • Analytic and vector geometry: straight lines, planes, circles, and spheres.

  2.   ANALYSIS, COMPLEX ANALYSIS, AND TOPOLOGY
         • Real analysis: the real number system, suprema and infima, sequences,
           subsequences, and Cauchy sequences.
         • Metric spaces: metric spaces, open and closed sets, convergence,
           completeness, and continuous mappings.
         • Topology: topological spaces, bases, open and closed sets and Hausdorff
           spaces.
         • Complex analysis: complex numbers, complex functions, limits, continuity,
           differentiability, analytic functions, and the Cauchy-Riemann equations.

  3.   ALGEBRA AND DISCRETE MATHEMATICS
         • Linear Algebra: matrices, determinants, systems of linear equations, rank,
           vector spaces, subspaces, basis, dimension, linear transformations,
           eigenvalues, eigenvectors.
         • Abstract algebra: binary operations, groups, subgroups, cyclic groups, cosets,
           and Lagrange's theorem etc.
         • Set Theory and discrete mathematics: sets, relations, functions, mathematical
           logic, and mathematical induction.

  4.   DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS AND MATHEMATICAL METHODS
          • Ordinary differential equations: first-order differential equations including
            separable, homogeneous, linear, Bernoulli and exact equations.
          • Partial differential equations: formation of partial differential equations
            (PDEs), first-order PDEs, and second-order PDEs.

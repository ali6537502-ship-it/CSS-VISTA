       COMBINED COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION (CCE) SYLLABUS

                            INTERNATIONAL AFFAIRS
                                  (Objective + Subjective)

Total Marks: 100

PART–A: INTERNATIONAL AFFAIRS
      Evolution of Global Geopolitical Order and Changing Power Structures
      Contemporary Global Order, Multipolarity & Great Power Competition
      Geopolitical and Geostrategic Dynamics of South Asia
      Globalization, Digital Diplomacy & Strategic Communication
      Climate Change, Emerging Technologies, Artificial Intelligence & Cyber Security
      International Political & Economic Institutions and their Reform
      Geopolitics of Energy, Maritime Chokepoints & Global Strategic Resources
      Global Power Transitions, Sanctions & International Economic Leverage
      Emerging Challenges to the Multilateral International Order

PART–B: FOREIGN POLICY OF PAKISTAN
      Pakistan’s Geostrategic and Geoeconomic Significance
      Determinants, Objectives & Evolution of Pakistan’s Foreign Policy
      Pakistan’s Relations with Neighbouring & Regional Countries
      Pakistan’s Relations with Muslim World & Islamic Organizations
      Pakistan’s Relations with Major Global Powers & Emerging Power Centres
      Pakistan’s Role in Regional & International Organizations
      Contemporary Global & Regional Issues Affecting Pakistan
      Pakistan’s Security, Kashmir, Water, Economic, Trade & Maritime Diplomacy
      Future Prospects & Emerging Challenges for Pakistan’s Foreign Policy

PART–C: NATIONAL SECURITY–EXTERNAL AND INTERNAL DYNAMICS
      Concept, Evolution and Dimensions of National Security
      Pakistan’s External Security Environment, Strategic Position & Defence Doctrine
      Armed Forces of Pakistan & Civil-Military Dimensions of National Security
      Internal Security: Extremism, Terrorism, Sectarianism, Crime & Civil Unrest
      National Response to Extremism & Terrorism
      National Action Plan, Paigham-e-Pakistan, National Counter Terrorism Authority
       (NACTA) and Countering Violent Extremism
      Economic, Energy, Water & Food Security
      Human, Environmental, Climate & Health Security
      Nuclear Deterrence & Strategic Stability
      Hybrid, Grey-Zone, Information & Emerging Forms of Warfare
      Artificial Intelligence, Cyber Security & Emerging Technologies
      Future & Non-Traditional Security Challenges for Pakistan & the International
       Community

Note: The topics mentioned in the syllabus are only indicative for the general
      guidance of the candidates and cannot be deemed as an exhaustive list.

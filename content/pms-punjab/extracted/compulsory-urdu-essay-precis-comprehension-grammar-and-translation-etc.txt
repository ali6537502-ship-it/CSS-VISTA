                      PUNJAB PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION
            COMBINED COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION (CCE) SYLLABUS
        URDU (ESSAY, PRÉCIS, COMPREHENSION, GRAMMAR AND TRANSLATION ETC.)

   Duration: 03 hours
   Total Marks: 100

1. Essay in Urdu:
   a)  Candidates would be required to write an essay in Urdu of about 1000 words. A choice of topics
       will be given in the question paper. The topics may include those on the issues related to education,
       economy, environment, foreign policy, governance, human rights, the Constitution, law and order
       and other socioeconomic issues related to Pakistan, as well as topics of general nature.
   b) The candidates are expected to reflect a clear understanding of the topic, conceptual clarity and
       logical reasoning. Candidates’ articulation, expression and correct use of Urdu language will be
       examined.
2. Urdu Précis and Comprehension: The examination will essentially test the candidates’ ability to handle
    précis writing, comprehension, syntax, translation, grammar, vocabulary, etc.
   a)    Précis Writing: A passage of a general nature with enough room for compression may be given for
         writing a précis and suggesting a suitable title.
   b)    Comprehension: A carefully selected passage, which is rich in substance but not specific to any
         discipline, may be given, followed by five questions requiring answers from the passage itself.
   c)    Grammar & Vocabulary: Questions relating to correct usage of tenses, articles, prepositions,
         conjunctions, punctuations, verbs, etc may be given.
   d)    Sentence Correction: Sentences may be given, each containing a grammatical or punctuation error
         etc. Candidates shall be expected to correct them without making unnecessary alterations.
   e)    Grouping of Words & Pair of Words: Words, chosen randomly, may be given that need to be
         grouped by candidates in pairs having similar or opposite meanings, use of idioms and proverbs as
         may be clearly directed in the question, antonyms, synonyms, etc.

3. Translation: A short passage in English containing figurative or idiomatic expressions may be
   given for translation into Urdu.




Note:   The above list is only suggestive and not exhaustive. Candidates may consult other standard
        books and relevant material covering the prescribed syllabus.

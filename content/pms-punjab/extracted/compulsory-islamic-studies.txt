               PUNJAB PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION
       COMBINED COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION (CCE) SYLLABUS

                                    ISLAMIC STUDIES
                                    (Objective + Subjective)
Total Marks: 100

1. Introduction to Islam

      Advent of Islam and its importance
      Basic Beliefs of Islam (Aqaid)
      Fundamentals of Islam (Arkan-e-Islam)
      Deen: its religious, spiritual, moral, and social impact on:
          o Individual
          o Society
      Amar Bilmaroof –wa–Nahi anil Munkar

2. Seerat-un-Nabi (PBUH)

      Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) as:
          o Messenger
          o Reformer
          o Administrator
          o Educator
          o Diplomat
          o Statesman
          o Military Leader

      Distinctive characteristics (Khasais) of the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH)

3. Early Islamic History

      The Prophetic Period
      The Era of Abu Bakr al-Siddiq (RA)
      The Era of Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA)
      The Era of Uthman ibn Affan (RA)
      The Era of Ali ibn Abi Talib (RA)

4. Human Rights in Islam

      Including:
           o Status of Women
           o Rights of Minorities
           o Objectives of Islam (Maqasid-ul-Shariah), with special reference to human rights
5. Islam and the World

         Islam’s impact on the West and the West’s influence on the Muslim world
         Islam’s role in the modern world
         Contemporary challenges for the Muslim world and Muslim minorities

6. Islamic Code of Life

         Salient features of:
              o Islamic socio-political system
              o Islamic economic system with particular reference to interest (riba)
              o Judicial system
              o Administrative system

 7. Sources of Islamic Law


Note:      The topics mentioned in the syllabus are only indicative for the general guidance of
           the candidates and cannot be deemed as an exhaustive list.

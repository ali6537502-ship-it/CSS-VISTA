             COMBINED COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION (CCE) SYLLABUS

                                       PSYCHOLOGY
Total Marks: 100

                                                 Topics
    1.   Introduction to Psychology
             • Definition, evolution, and scope of psychology
             • Schools of psychology
             • Recent trends in psychology
    2.   Sensation and Perception
             • Sensory processes
             • Five senses
             • Perception and laws of perceptual organization
             • Types of perception
             • Perceptual constancy
             • Optical illusions

    3.   Learning, Memory, and Intelligence
             • Learning: Definition, theories, and types of learning
             • Memory: Definition, theories, types of memory, and everyday forgetting
             • Intelligence: Definition, theories, and intelligence testing
    4.   Biological Basis of Behaviour
             • Structure and functions of the nervous system
             • Endocrine system
             • Brain-behaviour relationship
             • Nature-nurture controversy
    5.   Developmental Psychology
            • Physical, cognitive, social, and emotional development during childhood,
                adolescence, adulthood, and old age
            • Piaget’s theory of cognitive development


    6.   Motivation and Emotions
            • Motivation: definition and theories; primary and secondary motives; and the role
               of culture in motivation
            • Emotions: definition and theories ; and the biological and psychological
               changes associated with emotions
    7.   Health, Stress, and Coping
            • Health-impairing behaviours
            • Stress and coping strategies
            • Stress-illness relationship
            • Measurement of stress

         • Confessions

         • Deception
         • Contemporary issues in forensic psychology

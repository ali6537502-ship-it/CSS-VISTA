            COMBINED COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION (CCE) SYLLABUS


                                     GEOGRAPHY
Total Marks: 100
                                              Topics

1   PHYSICAL GEOGRAPHY: -
       The Universe, Earth-Sun relationships, Composition and internal structure of the Earth,
        Rocks and Their Types (Igneous, Sedimentary and Metamorphic), Plate tectonics
        (Causes and effects), Earthquakes, Volcanism and Their Landforms, Weathering
        processes, Mass movements, Landforms produced by Surface water/Streams, Karst
        processes, Wind and Glaciers (Degradation and aggradation).
       Atmospheric and surface temperature, Air pressure and winds (Circulation pattern of
        the Atmosphere), Precipitation, Air Masses and Fronts (Weather systems, weather
        tracking and forecasting)
       Human-Climate Interaction (Causes and effects, Urban Microclimates), Environmental
        Pollution (Air, water, soil and noise) and Global Warming (EL Nino-Southern
        Oscillation). Ocean currents and tides, World major biomes. Introduction to
        Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Remote Sensing (RS) and Global Positioning
        System (GPS), Topographical Maps and Weather maps of Pakistan.

2   HUMAN GEOGRAPHY: -
       World Human Population (Growth, Distribution, Density with reference to MDC’s and
        LDC’s), Population Dynamics (Change in size and structure driven by births, deaths and
        migration), Human Settlements with its types (Forms and functions), Urbanization
        (Hierarchy and Models of Urban Structures), Challenges of an Urban place, Green and
        Brown Agenda.

       Economic categories related to Production and consumption(Producer, consumer and
        decision making). Farming Types and Their distribution (Subsistence, intensive,
        commercial, mixed, dairying and luxury crops), Agricultural production (Wheat, rice,
        cotton and sugarcane), Food security and safety, Mineral resources (Metalliferous and
        non-metalliferous), Power resources (Renewable and non-renewable) with special
        reference to regional dynamics, Manufacturing (Factors of location, Iron and steel, high
        technology industries with special reference to emergence of new power zones),
        Transportation (Modes and International trade routes), Trade (World free trade zones,
        common market and pattern of the world’s trade).

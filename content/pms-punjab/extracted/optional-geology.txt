                       PUNJAB PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION
            COMBINED COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION (CCE) SYLLABUS

                                           GEOLOGY
Total Marks: 100

                                                    Topics
       1   General Geology: The Earth as a member of the Solar System. Origin, age, composition,
           and internal structure of the Earth. Major rock-forming minerals. Basic description and
           classification of igneous, sedimentary, and metamorphic rocks. Geomorphic processes,
           including internal and external processes. Valley formation and drainage patterns. Glacial
           processes and landforms.
       2   Structural Geology: Physical properties of rocks and their mechanical behavior under
           different tectonic conditions. Stress and strain and their relationship to rock deformation.
           Deformation through folding and fracturing. Major geological structures, including:
           Folds, Faults, Joints, Cleavage, Lineations and other linear
           structures. Structural interpretation in relation to tectonic environments.
       3   Paleontology: Fundamental principles and techniques of paleontology. Origin and
           evolution of life through geological time. Fossils and the process of fossilization. Modes
           of fossil preservation. Classification and identification of fossils. Biostratigraphic
           applications of fossils. Geological significance and environmental interpretation of
           fossils.
       4   Stratigraphy and Sedimentology: Fundamental principles of stratigraphy. Stratigraphic
           record and its interpretation. Geological Time Scale. Concepts of Sequence stratigraphy
           and its applications. Origin and sources of sediments. Weathering, erosion,
           transportation, and deposition of sediments. Sedimentary processes and sedimentary
           environments. Sedimentary structures and textures.
           Depositional environments and their interpretation. Importance of Sedimentology in
           natural resource exploration.
       5   Mineralogy, Petrology, and Geochemistry: Crystal chemistry and crystal structures.
           Crystal growth and mineral genesis. Mineral identification and classification. Stability
           and alteration of minerals. Mineralogical, chemical, textural, and structural characteristics
           of rocks. Origin, classification, and evolution of Igneous, Sedimentary and Metamorphic
           rocks. Chemical processes involved in the development and evolution of the Earth.
           Distribution and abundance of elements in the Earth's crust, atmosphere, and oceans.
           Stable and volatile elements and their geochemical behavior. Geochemical processes
           affecting minerals and rocks. Formation and stability of soil minerals.

       6   Earth Resources and Economic Geology: Classification, occurrence, exploration, and
           utilization of Earth's natural resources. Fossil fuels, including: Coal, Petroleum, Natural
           gas, Nuclear mineral resources. Uranium and strategic metals. Renewable energy
           resources. Hydropower resources. Geothermal energy. Water resources: Hydrological
           cycle Surface water and Groundwater. Construction materials, including: Concrete
           aggregates, Sand and gravel, Cement-making materials and Building stones.
           Fundamentals of metallogeny. Mineral deposits and their geological controls. Sustainable
           management of geological and natural resources.
                                                     Topics

     7    Fundamentals and scope of engineering geology: Engineering properties of soils and rocks.
          Principles of excavation in rock and soil. Landslides and their classification in rock and soil.
          Causes and mechanisms of slope failure. Basic principles of slope stability analysis. Site
          investigation and geological characterization. Geological considerations in the selection and
          evaluation of dam sites. Occurrence and movement of groundwater. Aquifers and aquifer
          properties. Groundwater recharge and discharge.
     8    Remote Sensing (RS) and Geographic Information Systems (GIS): Principles and
          applications of remote sensing and GIS. Earth satellite systems and satellite-based observations.
          Types of remotely sensed data and imagery. Basic principles of image interpretation. GIS data
          structures and data types. Spatial and attribute data. Methods of spatial data analysis. Data
          management and visualization. Cartographic modeling. Applications of RS and GIS in
          geological and environmental studies.

     9    Environmental Geology, Climate, and Climate Change: Introduction to environmental
          geology. Scientific basis of the climate system and climate change. Geological and
          environmental evidence of past climate change. Impacts of climate change on different regions
          of the world. Climate-related geological and environmental hazards. Adaptation and
          mitigation strategies. Relationship between Earth's geological processes and environmental
          change.
     10   Regional Geology and Geology of Pakistan: Geological framework and tectonic evolution
          of Pakistan. Major geological provinces and tectonic zones of Pakistan. Plate tectonics and its
          application to the geological evolution of Pakistan. Stratigraphy of the Upper, Middle and
          Lower Indus Basin. Major Basin of Pakistan. Mineral and energy resources of Pakistan.
          Metallogeny and mineral deposits of Pakistan. Geological significance of major dams and
          engineering
          projects in Pakistan. Major groundwater and surface-water resources of Pakistan.
     11   Information Technology, Artificial Intelligence, and Digital Applications in Geology:
          Applications of Information Technology in geological data collection, processing, analysis,
          and visualization. Introduction to digital geological mapping, databases, GIS, and remote
          sensing. Fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep
          Learning (DL). Applications of AI and ML in geological mapping, mineral exploration,
          geohydrology, engineering geology, and environmental studies. AI-based interpretation of
          geological, geochemical, geophysical, and remote-sensing data. Applications of drones,
          LiDAR, 3D geological modelling, and digital mapping.

                           PUNJAB PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION
            COMBINED COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION (CCE) SYLLABUS

     ENGLISH (PRÉCIS, COMPREHENSION, GRAMMAR AND TRANSLATION ETC.)

Duration: 03 Hours
Total Marks: 100

a.      English Précis and Comprehension: The examination will essentially test the ability of the
        candidates to handle précis writing, comprehension, syntax, translation, grammar, vocabulary etc.

        (i). Précis Writing: A passage of a generic nature with enough room for compression may be given
             for writing a précis and suggesting a suitable topic. (20 Marks)
        (ii). Comprehension: A carefully selected passage, which is rich in substance but not specific to any
             discipline, may be given followed by five questions requiring answers from the passage itself.
             (20 Marks)
        (iii). Grammar & Vocabulary: Questions relating to correct usage of tense, articles, prepositions,
             conjunctions, punctuation, verbs etc., may be given. (20 Marks)
        (iv). Sentence Correction: Sentences may be given, each having a flaw in grammar, punctuation or
             use of capitals, etc. Candidates shall be expected to correct them without making unnecessary
             alterations. (10 Marks)
        (v). Grouping of Words & Pairs of Words: Words, chosen randomly, may be given that need to
             be grouped by candidates in pairs having similar or opposite meaning, use of idioms and
             proverbs as may be clearly directed in the question, antonyms, synonyms, etc. (10 Marks)

b.      Translation: A short passage in Urdu that may contain figurative or idiomatic expressions may be
        given for an accurate translation into English. (20 Marks)

Note: Marks allocated for each component are indicative and subject to change.

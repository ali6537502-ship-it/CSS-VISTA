               PUNJAB PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION
        COMBINED COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION (CCE) SYLLABUS

                   ETHICS FOR NON-MUSLIM CANDIDATES
                                 (Objective + Subjective)
Total Marks: 100


        1. Meaning, Definition, and Scope of Ethics

        2. Relation of Ethics with:
                Religion
                Law
                Society & Human Beings
                Science


        3. The Development of Human Moral Conduct
                Instinctive Level of Morality
                Customary Level of Morality
                Reflective Level of Morality


        4. Moral Theories
               Utilitarianism (Mill)
               Idealism (Green)
               Kant’s Moral Theory


        5. Moral Ethics and Society
               Freedom and Responsibility
               Tolerance
               Justice
               Punishment (Theories of Punishment)


        6. Moral Teachings of Major Religions
               Christianity
               Islam
               Hinduism
               Buddhism
               Sikhism
               Judaism
               Zoroastrianism


Note:   The topics mentioned in the syllabus are only indicative for the general guidance
        of the candidates and cannot be deemed as an exhaustive list.

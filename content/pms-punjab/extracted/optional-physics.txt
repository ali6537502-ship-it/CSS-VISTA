                    PUNJAB PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION
          COMBINED COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION (CCE) SYLLABUS

                                             PHYSICS
    Total Marks: 100
                                                Topics
1   Mechanics and Special Relativity
    Scalars and vectors; Newton’s laws of motion; conservation of energy, linear momentum, and
    angular momentum; circular and rotational motion; gravitation and satellite motion; Basic
    concepts of special relativity, Lorentz transformations, time dilation, length contraction and
    mass–energy equivalence.
2   Fluid Mechanics, Oscillations, Waves and Optics
    Surface tension, viscosity, and Bernoulli’s theorem; simple harmonic motion and forced
    oscillations; travelling and standing waves; Basic wave phenomena, including
    diffraction and polarization; electromagnetic waves; lasers and their applications.
3   Heat, Thermodynamics and Statistical Mechanics
    Ideal gases and the van der Waals equation; laws of thermodynamics; entropy and basic
    thermodynamic potentials; kinetic theory of gases and Maxwell’s velocity distribution; heat
    transfer; introductory concepts of Maxwell–Boltzmann, Bose–Einstein, and Fermi–Dirac
    statistics.
4   Electricity and Magnetism
    Electric field, electric potential, and Gauss’s law; capacitance and dielectrics; magnetic fields
    and Ampère’s law; magnetic properties of matter; electromagnetic induction; Alternating-
    current circuits and resonance; Maxwell’s equations and the basic properties of
    electromagnetic waves.
5   Electronics and Digital Systems
    Semiconductor basics; p-type and n-type semiconductors; p–n junction diodes and rectification;
    transistors and their basic applications; operational amplifiers and elementary applications;
    number systems, Boolean algebra and basic logic gates.
6   Atomic Physics and Quantum Mechanics
    Bohr’s atomic model; quantum numbers, electron spin, and Pauli’s exclusion principle;
    photoelectric effect and Compton scattering; de Broglie hypothesis and Heisenberg’s
    uncertainty principle; basic Schrödinger wave equation and its applications; Zeeman effect.
7   Nuclear and Particle Physics
    Nuclear structure, mass defect and binding energy; radioactive decay and half-life; alpha, beta,
    and gamma radiation; detection of nuclear radiation; nuclear reactions, fission and fusion;
    Basic principles of nuclear reactors; elementary particles and their
    fundamental properties.
8   Solid State Physics
    Crystal structures and lattice concepts; energy-band theory; conductors, insulators and
    semiconductors; Basic magnetic properties of solids; superconductivity and its applications.

                PUNJAB PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION
       COMBINED COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION (CCE) SYLLABUS

                                      PERSIAN
Total Marks: 100
INSTRUCTIONS: The question paper shall consist of four sections, each containing two
questions, making a total of eight questions. Candidate shall attempt any five questions,
selecting at least one question from each section. All questions shall carry equal marks of
20 each, making a total of 100 marks.

                                         Part-I
HISTORY OF PERSIAN LITERATURE IN IRAN, AFGHANISTAN AND
CENTRAL ASIA
                                         Topics
1.       a) Samanid Period (‫)سامانی‬
         b) Ghaznavid Period (‫)غزنوی‬
         c) Seljuk Period (‫)سلجوقی‬
         d) Ilkhanid Period (‫)ایلخانی‬
         e) Timurid Period (‫)تیموری‬
         f) Safavid Period (‫)صفوی‬
         g) Qajar Period (‫)قاجاری‬
         h) Persian Literature in the Twentieth Century (‫)بیسویں صدی کا فارسی ادب‬

                                         Part-II
     HISTORY OF PERSIAN LITERATURE IN THE SUBCONTINENT
                                      Topics
2.       a) Persian Literature during the Ghaznavid Period in the Subcontinent.
         b) Persian Literature during the Delhi Sultanate.
         c) Persian Literature during the Mughal Period.
         d) Persian Literature from 1857 to 1947.
         e) Persian Literature in the Subcontinent after 1947.
         f) The Role of Persian in the Formation and Development of the Urdu
            Language.

                        PUNJAB PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION
              COMBINED COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION (CCE) SYLLABUS

                                           STATISTICS
Total Marks: 100

                                                 Topics
                                               Section- A
 1.   Descriptive Statistics:
      Meaning of Statistics, its characteristics, uses and misuses of statistics, observations and
      variables, types of variables, data and its types according to nature (qualitative and
      quantitative), w.r.t. source (primary and secondary), measurement scales (nominal, ordinal,
      interval and ratio), population and sample, descriptive and inferential statistics, description of
      qualitative and quantitative data using frequency tables and graphs (simple bar chart,
      histogram, pie chart, stem and leaf plot), measures of central tendency and their types.
      Ungrouped and Grouped Data: arithmetic mean, median and mode, empirical relationship
      among mean, median and mode, properties of arithmetic mean (proof not required), weighted
      mean and its application.
      Ungrouped Data: Geometric mean and harmonic mean, Quantiles (quartiles, deciles and
      percentiles), relative merits and demerits of all above averages, measure of dispersion,
      absolute and relative measure of dispersion: Range, semi-interquartile range, variance and
      standard deviation, properties of variance and standard deviation (proof not required),
      coefficient of quartile deviation, coefficient of variation, Box and whisker plot, standardized
      variables.
 2.   Concept of probability and Random variables:
      Random experiment, sample space and events, counting principles, including the
      multiplication principle, combinations, and permutations, introduction to probability and
      axioms of probability, basic laws of probability, addition and multiplication laws of
      probability for two events, conditional probabilities, Bayes’ theorem (proof not required) and
      its application, discrete random variable, probability mass function and cumulative
      distribution function, joint distribution of two discrete random variables, marginal and
      conditional distribution, continuous random variable, probability density function and
      cumulative distribution function, joint distribution of two continuous random variables,
      marginal and conditional distribution, mathematical expectations and its properties, mean,
      variance, covariance and correlation of two discrete and continuous random variables.
 3.   Probability Distributions:
      Discrete Binomial, hypergeometric, Poisson distribution, application and fitting to real life
      data, concept of negative binomial and geometric distribution, continuous exponential and
      normal distributions, applications and fitting to real-life data, properties of normal distribution.

4.   Regression and Correlation Analysis:
     Concept of regression and correlation analysis, scatter diagram and its uses, estimation of
     simple linear regression, properties of the least-squares regression line, standard error of
     estimate, coefficient of determination, linear correlation, coefficient of correlation and its
     properties. Spearman’s rank correlation (with and without tied observations), partial and
     multiple correlations up to three variables.
5.   Index Numbers and Basics of Time series:
     Construction and application of wholesale price index numbers, fixed and chain base methods,
     weighted      index     numbers        (Laspeyres’,   Paasche’s,     Fisher’s    ideal    and
     Marshall-Edgeworth indices), tests for the consistency of Index Numbers, construction of
     consumer price index numbers, sensitive price indicator, components of a time series, analysis
     of time series, measurement of secular trend and seasonal variations by various methods,
     deseasonalization of data.
                                                Topics
                                               Section-B
6    Sampling and Sampling Distribution:
     Concept of sampling, advantages and scope of sampling, probability and non probability
     sampling, sampling and non sampling error, sampling design and sampling frame, sampling
     design of simple random sampling, stratified, systematic and cluster sampling, judgment and
     quota sampling, sampling distributions of sample means, proportions and variances under
     simple random sampling, sampling distributions of mean of stratified random sampling,
     sampling distributions of difference between two sample means and the sample proportions,
     and their properties, concept of central limit theorem, concepts of t-, 𝜒 2 -, F-distributions.
7    Estimation and Testing of Hypothesis:
     Estimates and estimators, point estimator and its properties: Unbiasedness, consistency,
     efficiency and sufficiency, interval estimation and its interpretations, interval estimation of the
     single population mean or proportion with known and unknown variances, differences
     between two mean/proportions with known and unknown variances, determination of sample
     size, interval estimation of population variance and ratio of two population variances, null and
     alternative hypothesis, simple and composite hypothesis, concept of Type-I and Type-II error,
     level of significance, concept of p-value, acceptance and rejection regions, one sided and two
     sided test, testing of hypothesis for single mean/proportion, difference between two
     means/proportions, testing of hypothesis (based on small sample and unknown population
     variance) for single mean, difference between two means, paired and independent
     observations, testing of hypothesis about the variances and equality of two variances, concept
     of association, contingency tables, chi-square test for independence of attributes.
8    Analysis of variance and Experimental Design:
     Definition, importance and assumptions of analysis of variance, partitioning of sums of
     squares and degrees of freedom in one and two way classification, testing the equality of
     means for one and two way classification, multiple comparison tests: least significant
     difference test, Duncan’s multiple range test, principles of experimental design, completely
     randomized, randomized complete block and Latin square design, description of the layout,

      9     Non-Parametric Tests:
            Parametric vs Non-parametric tests, when to use non-parametric procedures, testing
            procedures and applications one-sample tests: Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test, Run test, testing
            procedures and applications two related-sample tests: Wilcoxon Signed Rank Test, testing
            procedures and applications for two independent samples: Mann-Whitney U test, run test,
            testing procedures and application two or more independent samples: Kruskal Wallis H test.
      10    Vital Statistics and Official Statistics:
            Vital events, uses and shortcomings of vital statistics, sources of demographic; data gender
            and child woman ratio, vital index, crude, specific and standardised death and birth rates,
            general fertility rate, gross and net reproduction rate, introduction to official statistics, sources
            of official statistics, use of official statistics in administration and planning, concepts and
            evaluation of GDP, GNP, NNP, registration systems of births and deaths in Pakistan.

                PUNJAB PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION
      COMBINED COMPETITIVE EXAMINATION (CCE) SYLLABUS

                                        CHEMISTRY
Total Marks: 100
                                                 Topics
1.   Physical Chemistry: Quantum theory, The Schrödinger wave equation, Quantum Numbers,
     Eigen values and eigen functions, First Law of thermodynamic and Enthalpy changes, Entropy
     and second law of thermodynamics, Debye-Huckel theory and its applications for strong
     electrolytes, Electrodes, electrode potential, and its measurement.
2.   Organic Chemistry: Inductive effect, delocalized chemical bonding and the resonance effect,
     tautomerism, hyper-conjugation, steric effect and hydrogen bonding, Preparation and
     properties of catalyzed aldol condensation carbonyl group, Acid and base reactions and
     nucleophilic additions to the carbonyl group, Stereoisomerism, conformational analysis of
     cycloalkanes, chirality and optical activity.
3.   Inorganic Chemistry: Valence bond theory, Hybridization of orbitals, Molecular Orbital
     theory, Shapes of inorganic molecules, Applications of the VSEPR theory, General
     Characteristics of d-block elements, Transition metal complexes, structure of coordinate
     complex compounds, postulates and applications of Werner’s Chelates, Nomenclature and
     isomerism in coordinate compounds.
4.   Analytical Chemistry: Fundamentals of chemometrics, Principle of UV-visible and IR
     spectroscopy, bathochromic shift, hypsochromic shift, Instrumentation and working of UV-
     Visible and IR Spectrophotometers, Principle and types of chromatography. Thin-layer and
     column chromatography with their applications.
5.   Biochemistry: Introduction, classification, structure, and metabolism of carbohydrates.
     Primary, secondary, and Tertiary structure of proteins, lipids, and vitamins.
6.   Environmental Chemistry: Environmental Pollution, Greenhouse effect, Air Pollution, Water
     Pollution and Chemical Toxicology, Renewable Energy Resources
7.   Industrial Chemistry: Sulphuric acid, chemical fertilizers, cement, and ceramics, Soda ash
     and caustic soda, Detergents, Pesticides, Cosmetics and Pigments
8.   Nuclear Chemistry: Radioactivity, its detection, and its measurement, kinetics of radioactive
     decay, Nuclear Fission, Nuclear Fusion, Artificial Radioactivity, uses of radioactive isotopes
     and nuclear reactors

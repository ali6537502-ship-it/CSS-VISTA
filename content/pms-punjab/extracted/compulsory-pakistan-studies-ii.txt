                                  PAKISTAN STUDIES – II
                                      (Objective + Subjective)
     Total Marks: 100

                                             PART-A

I.       PAKISTANI SOCIETY AND CULTURE

1.       Pakistani Society
        Social stratification: Class, caste, biradari, tribe, status, and social mobility
        Social institutions: Family, school, religion, and media
        Rural and urban society: Features, culture, and lifestyles
        Approaches to studying Pakistani society
        Human Resources of Pakistan: Demographic, ethnic, linguistic and sectarian composition

2.       Pakistani Culture
        Major regional cultures: Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, Kashmir, and
         Gilgit-Baltistan
        Unity in diversity: Similarities and differences
        Common cultural patterns and themes
        Cultural strengths, weaknesses, and ethno-linguistic challenges
        Factors promoting and hindering national integration
        Importance of local languages

3.       Modernization and Its Impact on Society
        Urbanization and its impact
        Internal and international migration: positive and negative consequences
        Agricultural modernization and its effect on rural social systems
        Industrialization, technology, and the role of education and elites in social change
        Transformation from traditional to modern society
        Crisis of cultural identity and ethnic/national issues

4.       Social Problems
        Poverty, unemployment, old age, alienation, crime, feuds, conservatism
        Gender inequality and status of women
        Environmental degradation and overpopulation
        Illiteracy and challenges in education
        Drug Peddling and Child Labour
        Terrorism, religious/sectarian militancy, corruption

         Questions of nationalities and ethnicity
         Cultural identity and its challenges


                                             PART – B:

                                      LAND AND PEOPLE
I.        GEOGRAPHY

1.        Physical Geography of Pakistan
         Introduction: strategic location, neighbours and administrative divisions
         Emergence of Pakistan: (ideological, cultural, social, economic causes)
         Major landforms: mountains, plains, rivers, plateaus and deserts
         Climatic conditions: (temperature, rainfall, pressure and winds, climatic regions)
         Geographical factors affecting climate
         Natural vegetation and soils

2.        Human Geography of Pakistan
         Demographic profile: population growth, density and distribution, literacy, urban-rural
          distribution
         Human settlements: cities and towns, urbanization and challenges of urban change
         Population-related issues and problems
         Human resources

3.        Economic and Commercial Geography of Pakistan
         Agriculture: major crops and livestock, irrigation system and related problems
         Industry: heavy, light and cottage industries; contribution to national economy
         Trade: Internal and foreign trade (imports, exports and trade partners)
         Transport system: Means of transportation and its importance
         Natural resources: Renewable and non-renewable, their economic utilization for national
          development

II.       CLIMATIC CHANGE AND DISASTER RISK MANAGEMENT

1.        Climatic Change
         Introduction (Climate change and global warming)
         Causes of Climatic Change

             o   Burning of fossil fuels
             o   Deforestation and Land-use change,
             o   Agriculture and Livestock Farming
             o   Industrial processes and manufacturing
             o   Waste management and Landfills
                                                                                           Page 2 of 5
        Impacts of Climatic Change

            o   Rising temperature
            o   Intense Extreme weather
            o   Severe droughts and water scarcity
            o   Threats to food and agriculture
            o   Public health risks

2.       Disaster Risk Management in Pakistan

        Introduction: Hazards and disasters
        Types of Disasters
             o Natural disasters: earthquakes, floods, landslides, drought, heat waves, cloud burst
             o Man-made disasters: industrial accidents, urban flooding, smog, urban fire, urban
                 heat island
        Causes and Challenges
             o Lack of coordination among organizations
             o Shortage of trained human resources
             o Limited technology and haphazard planning
             o Poor governance
        Effects of Disasters
             o Visible losses: Human losses, property, infrastructure
             o Invisible losses: psychological trauma, social disruption, diseases
        Social Impacts
             o Victim support challenges
             o Gender-related and child-related issues
        Disaster Risk Management Techniques
             o Seismic hazard assessment
             o Risk assessment and risk management
             o Land-use planning
             o Early warning and forecasting
             o Pre-disaster preparedness
             o During-disaster action
             o Post-disaster management
        Preparedness and Mitigation
             o Capacity building and coordination
             o Volunteer organization
             o National policy for reconstruction, relief, and rehabilitation
